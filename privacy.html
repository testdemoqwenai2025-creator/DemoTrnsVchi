Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2026-09-22. This notice describes how the AV/Robotics Operations Center handles personal data, in alignment with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

🍪 1. Cookies

This application uses only essential cookies — specifically, a single class of cookie/localStorage entry to remember your theme preference (dark / light / system) and, if you check "Remember me" on the demo login form, a second entry to remember your demo session between visits.

We do not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, social-media cookies, or any third-party tracking cookies. There is no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no advertising network, and no cross-site tracking on this site.

You can clear these entries at any time using your browser's developer tools (Application → Local Storage → clear) or by signing out from the header.

🗄 2. Data we collect

We collect no personal data from your visit beyond what you explicitly type into the demo login form (an email address and a password). That information is stored only in your browser's localStorage on your own device and is never transmitted to a server in this demo build.

The application is a multi-page demo. There is no backend database in this build. All fleet, mission, telemetry, and diagnostics data shown in the UI is generated deterministically by an in-process mock data layer — it does not represent real users, real vehicles, or real telemetry.

Server logs (if any are produced by your hosting provider) are not controlled by this application and would be governed by your hosting provider's privacy notice.

👤 3. Your rights under GDPR

Under the GDPR you have the following rights with respect to any personal data:

  • Right of access — you can ask what data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — you can ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure — you can ask us to delete your data.
  • Right to data portability — you can ask for your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — you can object to processing for specific purposes.
  • Right to withdraw consent — at any time, for any processing based on consent.

Because this demo stores data only in your own browser, you can exercise all of these rights directly by clearing your browser storage. For any formal request, contact us at the email below.

🛡 4. Legal basis & data controller

The legal basis for processing in this demo is your explicit consent (given when you sign in to the demo form). No processing is necessary for the performance of a contract, for compliance with a legal obligation, or for the protection of vital interests, because no real-world contract or legal obligation exists in this demo build.

The data controller for this application is the project owner reachable at the contact email below. No data processor is engaged, because no data leaves your browser.
