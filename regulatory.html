Regulatory Compliance

Regulatory bodies require transparent audit trails for safety-critical failures and manual safety-driver overrides. This page documents which standards the platform targets and what compliance evidence the UI must surface.

📄 Quarterly reporting cadence

Regulators require quarterly reporting. The report must include:

  1. Disengagement summary (count, causes, locations, time-of-day distribution)
  2. ODD deviations (times vehicle operated outside declared ODD)
  3. Safety-critical alerts (count by severity, root-cause analysis)
  4. OTA updates applied (version history, what changed, rollback events)
  5. Crash incidents (if any, with NHTSA SGO 24-hour report attachments)
  6. Audit trail integrity (proof that black-box logs are unmodified — hash chain)
