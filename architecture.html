🛡 ASIL compliance notes
● Rows 4 and 8 are ASIL-D — the safety-critical path. The UI displays their state but is not safety-rated; the UI failing must never affect the safety kernel's operation.
● Data flow from AURIX to UI is one-way read-only at the boundary. The UI can never write to the safety kernel.
● Rows 1–3, 6, 7 are ASIL-QM — quality-managed but not safety-critical. The UI can display and write back through approved APIs.
🗄 Why rows 1–4, 6–8 are out of scope
Hardware dependency: rows 1, 3, 8 require Jetson AGX Orin and Infineon AURIX TC3xx — not available in any web sandbox.
Real-time OS dependency: rows 2, 4 require PREEMPT_RT Linux on x86 — not available in a Next.js sandbox.
Specialist languages: rows 6 (Golang) and 7 (Scala/Spark) would mean maintaining three separate codebases.
IP / customer stack: rows 1–4 typically belong to the customer's AV stack. We provide the UI; they provide the vehicle.
